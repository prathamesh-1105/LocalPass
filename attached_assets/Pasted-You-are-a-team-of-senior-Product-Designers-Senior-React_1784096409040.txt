-------------------------------------------------

LocalOne is a digital platform where students apply for Railway Student Concession.

Instead of paper forms:

Student submits application digitally.

College verifies digitally.

College approves digitally.

Digital concession certificate is generated.

Student tracks everything inside the app.

Future versions can integrate with Indian Railways.
